# Candidate instructions

As part of the second round of the interview process, we’d like you to complete a technical exercise before our live session.

Please review the requirements in `TICKET.md` carefully and make sure you fully understand what is being requested. You can work on the exercise whenever your schedule permits prior to the live interview.

## For the exercise

Please:

- Break the requirements in `TICKET.md` into clear, logical feature-level tasks.
- Implement the requested functionality.
- Create separate PRs for each feature/task where practical. We’re interested not only in the completed solution, but also in how you organize and structure the work.
- Include any appropriate notes, assumptions, or implementation details in your PRs that help explain your decisions.

## During the live session

We’ll ask you to walk us through your work. Please be prepared to:

### Explain your PRs and implementation approach

Walk us through how you interpreted the requirements in `TICKET.md`, how you broke the work down, the decisions you made, and any trade-offs or assumptions you considered.

### Demo the complete application

Show us the application running end-to-end and demonstrate the features you implemented. Please explain what each feature does, how it functions, and how it relates back to the requirements.

### Build a small feature together

If time permits, we’ll work collaboratively during the live session to add one more simple feature on top of what you have already built. This is intended to give us an opportunity to see how you approach a new requirement, think through changes, and collaborate in real time.

## Tools

You are welcome to use Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Antigravity, Kiro, or any combination of AI tools, IDEs, and development tools you prefer. Using AI is completely acceptable for this exercise. We are primarily interested in your understanding of the solution, the decisions you make, and your ability to explain and work with the code you produce.

## Time

There is no expectation that you spend an excessive amount of time on the exercise. Focus on producing a clear, functional solution that you can confidently explain and discuss during the live session.

Looking forward to seeing your approach and discussing it with you.
